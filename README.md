# Christmas elf ideas generator: 60 quick ideas and a 24-night plan

A free tool for parents doing a Christmas elf at home. Pick how much time you've got tonight (2, 5 or 10 minutes) and the kind of idea (Funny, Cheeky, Kind or Craft), tick "no mess only" if you need to, and get one idea in a tap. Or build a whole 1 to 24 December 2026 plan you can print or save as a PDF.

**Use it now, no install:** [tchworks.co.uk/free/christmas-elf-ideas-generator](https://www.tchworks.co.uk/free/christmas-elf-ideas-generator/?utm_source=github&utm_campaign=elf-repo)

## What's in this repo

- `index.html`: the whole tool in one file. Open it in a browser; there's no build step and nothing to install.
- `ideas.json`: all 60 ideas as data (name, idea, minutes, whether it's a little messy, type, what you need), MIT licensed, so you can reuse them in your own app, calendar or printable.
- The full list is also below, so you can just read it.

## How the 24-night plan works

- Night 1 (1 December) is an arrival letter and night 24 (Christmas Eve) is a goodbye letter.
- The six 10-minute ideas go on the weekend nights of 5-6, 12-13 and 19-20 December 2026, when there's more time.
- Every other night gets a different 2 or 5-minute idea, so no idea repeats.

## Privacy

Nothing is sent or saved. The ideas and the plan are generated in your browser.

### 2-minute ideas

- **Zip-wire elf** (Funny). Run string between two chair backs and hang the elf from it with a peg, mid-zip. *You need: string, a peg.*

- **Stuck in the biscuit tin** (Cheeky). Elf is head-first in the biscuit tin with just the legs showing. *You need: biscuit tin.*
- **Kindness note** (Kind). Elf leaves a note asking your child to do one kind thing for someone today. *You need: paper, pen.*
- **Hide and seek** (Funny). Elf hides with a sign saying 'Bet you can't find me before breakfast'. *You need: paper.*
- **Wrapped the remote** (Cheeky). Elf has wrapped the TV remote in wrapping paper with a 'no telly till tea' tag. *You need: wrapping paper, tape.*

- **Reading a bedtime story** (Kind). Elf sits on the bed holding a Christmas book open, ready for tonight's story. *You need: a book.*
- **Hanging from the light** (Cheeky). Elf dangles from a lampshade or light pull by a ribbon (not a hot bulb). *You need: ribbon.*
